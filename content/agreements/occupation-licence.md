# Home occupation licence

Draft for an Indian lawyer to review before any real member, document, or rupee is accepted. This is a revocable licence to occupy. It is not a lease and it does not create a tenancy.

**Home.** {{homeName}}, {{address}}, {{neighbourhood}}, {{city}}.
**Host.** {{hostName}}.
**Guest.** {{guestName}}, together with {{guestNames}}.
**Dates.** From {{start}} to {{end}}, {{nights}} nights. Check-in is with the {{handover}}.
**Keys.** {{keyCount}} key or keys. A lost key is paid for by the guest at cost.
**Points.** {{points}} points for the stay, at {{nightly}} points a night. Points are held when this licence is signed and the deposit is recorded. They move to the host at check-in, or 24 hours after the start if nobody has reported that the guest did not arrive.
**Deposit.** {{deposit}}, refundable. It is not rent. It is recorded through the payment aggregator and returned to the same payment method if no claim is open 72 hours after checkout. A claim needs photographs. The other person has 48 hours to answer. The desk can apply the deposit up to its cap. Anything above the cap is between the two members.
**What is included.** Ordinary use of the utilities. The guest pays for damage and for extraordinary charges.
**What stays shut.** {{excludedAreas}}
**House rules, and the society conditions the guest has accepted.** {{houseRules}}
**People.** No extra guests, no events, no commercial shoot, and no passing the home to anyone else.
**Host’s word.** The host can grant possession for these dates. The permissions on the file are valid. The home matches the listing.
**Guest’s word.** The identity on the file is true. The guest is responsible for the people named here.
**If the stay breaks.** Illegal use, extra guests, a society complaint, or damage lets the host end this licence. The guest leaves within 24 hours. The desk can suspend either member.
**The desk.** Aangan mediates the deposit up to its cap. It is not the occupier and it does not insure the home.
**Cancellation before check-in.** More than 14 days before the start, the points return to the guest. From 3 to 14 days, one night stays with the host and the rest returns. Under 3 days, or if the guest does not arrive, the whole hold stays with the host. The deposit returns if the guest has not stayed. If the host cancels, the points and the deposit return to the guest.
**Papers.** Identity numbers are not printed on the society pack. The pack carries names, dates, the kind of identity, a vehicle if there is one, and the phone numbers needed at the gate.
**Law.** Counsel will set the governing law, the court, and any stamp or registration this writing needs, including in Maharashtra, before launch.

Signed by the host as {{hostSigned}} and by the guest as {{guestSigned}}.
